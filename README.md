## About

10+ years of backend engineering spanning fintech, healthtech, and e-commerce. Core strengths in **Elixir/OTP** and **Ruby on Rails**, with deep expertise in WordPress/WooCommerce plugin architecture. I build systems that handle real-world load, integrate payment rails (M-Pesa, Stripe, Paystack), and solve actual problems for people across East Africa. Currently at **Araya Tech**, building tooling at the intersection of AI and business intelligence.

---

## Career Highlights

- Led the engineering team that built and deployed the **Kenya National Police Service biometric medical management system** across 1,000+ hospitals nationwide.
- Served as **Lead Technical Support Engineer for Advanced Custom Fields (ACF)**, the WordPress plugin powering millions of sites globally.
- Led the **WooCommerce HPOS migration** that doubled order throughput and significantly grew customer capacity for Greenspoon Kenya.
- Built multi-tenant SaaS platforms on **Elixir/Phoenix/OTP** with M-Pesa Daraja, Airtel Money, and Paystack payment rail integrations.
